# Product description and launch copy

## Short description

FreeBoard is a calm, private whiteboard for learning and thinking. Sketch ideas, explain lessons, arrange notes and connect a simple process—right in your browser, with no account or setup.

## Launch headline

Make space for ideas.

## Supporting copy

From the first sketch to the next big idea, FreeBoard keeps your canvas close and your tools simple. Draw smoothly, write in বাংলা or English, add images and shape a thought into something you can share.

Your boards save on this device. Download editable backups, export PNG or SVG artwork, and keep creating offline after the app is cached.

## Call to action

Open your whiteboard

## Launch post

Meet FreeBoard: a quiet space for lessons, brainstorms and everyday ideas. Open it and start drawing—no registration, payment or API key. Smooth pen strokes, editable notes, connected arrows, Bengali and English text, local images, and useful templates are ready when you are. Save on your device and take your ideas with you as an editable backup, PNG or SVG.

## Product promises

- Start immediately in the browser.
- Keep board contents on your device.
- Work offline after the app confirms it is cached.
- Download portable backups of important work.

Do not advertise real-time collaboration, cloud sync, unlimited storage, guaranteed browser retention or verified physical-device performance. Those capabilities are not part of this release.
