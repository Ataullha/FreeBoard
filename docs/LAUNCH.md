# Product description and launch copy

## Short description

FreeBoard is a calm, private whiteboard for learning and thinking. Sketch ideas, explain lessons, arrange notes and connect a simple process—right in your browser, with no account or setup.

## Launch headline

Make space for ideas.

## LinkedIn launch post

Good ideas often start with a blank board.

I’ve been working on FreeBoard—a free, open-source whiteboard for teaching, sketching, and thinking through ideas in the browser.

Open it and start drawing. No account or setup required.

It includes pressure-sensitive pen input, Bengali and English text, sticky notes, images, shapes, and attached arrows. Recent improvements add finer pen-width controls, a small clock, undoable board clearing, and a mouse eraser that grows when held.

Boards save locally on your device. You can download an editable backup, export PNG or SVG, and use the app offline after it has cached.

There’s also a new interactive product preview: try a lesson, brainstorm, or flowchart before opening your own board.

## Product promises

- Start immediately in the browser.
- Keep board contents on your device.
- Work offline after the app confirms it is cached.
- Download portable backups of important work.

Do not advertise real-time collaboration, cloud sync, unlimited storage, guaranteed browser retention or verified physical-device performance. Those capabilities are not part of this release.
