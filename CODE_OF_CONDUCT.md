# Code of Conduct

FreeBoard welcomes participation from people of every background and experience level. Be respectful, patient and constructive. Explain disagreements with evidence, focus on the work, and help newcomers learn.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized content, spam, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not pressure others to share private boards, credentials or personal details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. For a moderation concern, open an issue with only the minimum non-sensitive information and request a private follow-up before sharing personal details. Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
